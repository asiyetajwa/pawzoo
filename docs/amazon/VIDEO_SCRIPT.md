# Demo Film

77 seconds, 1920x1080. Built with Remotion in [`demo/`](../demo) from captures of the running apps (`npm run capture`), narrated by the ElevenLabs voice Jessica over an ElevenLabs Music lo-fi bed, ending on an ElevenLabs Sound Effects bark. The lines live in [`demo/src/pawzoo/script.ts`](../demo/src/pawzoo/script.ts); `npm run voiceover` regenerates them and `npm run render` cuts the film, each scene stretched to its line.

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pawzoo logo and "Your dog's day, on Alexa+". | This is Pawzoo. It turns your Ring cameras into a helper for your dog, right inside Alexa plus. |
| 2 | Simulator: tap "How Was Biscuit's Day?". The day card: walk, meals, food, moments, Play on TV and Reorder Food. | Just ask how Biscuit's day went. Pawzoo reads the day off your cameras and sums it up: her walk, her meals, and when her food runs out. |
| 3 | Tap "The Walk". The walk card, checked against the front door camera. | Tap the walk, and Pawzoo checks it against the front door camera. Dana kept her out for thirty-five minutes, and this week only Tuesday came up short. |
| 4 | Tap "Pay + $3 Tip", then Confirm. The receipt. | Pay Dana and add a tip right from the card. Pawzoo shows you the total first, and nothing is paid until you say yes. |
| 5 | Tap "Do We Need Food?", then "2.5 Cups a Day Now". The forecast moves. | Running low on food? Pawzoo tracks the bag and offers a reorder before it's empty. Tell it she's eating less now, and the forecast updates. |
| 6 | Tap "Book Grooming". Saturday 9:30 AM comes first. | Need grooming? It finds open slots and puts Saturday morning first, because you told it that is when Biscuit is calmest. |
| 7 | The Fire TV home, then the reel, chapter by chapter. | In the evening, the family gathers round Fire TV to relive her day. Pawzoo cuts the camera footage into a keepsake reel, one chapter for every moment, so the little things she did today become memories you get to keep. |
| 8 | The dog gets out: the simulator and the TV switch to the alert together. | And if Biscuit ever slips out the front door alone, Pawzoo speaks up right away, on your speaker and your TV. |
| 9 | Pawzoo, then "Ring sees. Alexa+ acts. Fire TV shows." and a bark. | Pawzoo. Ring sees, Alexa plus acts, and Fire TV shows. |
